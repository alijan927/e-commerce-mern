Our Journey

From a small startup to a leading e-commerce platform, here's our story

Founded in 2018, ShopEasy began with a simple idea: to create an online shopping experience that's truly easy for everyone. What started as a small team of passionate individuals has grown into a platform serving millions of customers worldwide.

2018

The Beginning

ShopEasy was founded by three friends who saw an opportunity to simplify online shopping. We started with just 50 products and a commitment to exceptional customer service.

2019

First Milestone

Reached 10,000 customers and expanded our product catalog to 1,000 items. Introduced our loyalty program and mobile app.

2020

Rapid Growth

Expanded to international markets. Launched same-day delivery in major cities and introduced AI-powered product recommendations.

2021

Innovation

Introduced AR product previews and sustainable packaging initiatives. Partnered with 500+ brands and reached 1 million customers.

2022

Recognition

Awarded "Best E-commerce Platform" by TechReview. Expanded our team to 200+ employees across 5 countries.

2023

Today & Beyond

Serving 2.5 million customers with 50,000+ products. Continuously innovating to provide the best shopping experience while maintaining our core values.

Our journey continues as we work towards our vision of becoming the world's most customer-centric e-commerce platform.
